Profile

About

Dr. Usman Qayyum is a researcher, AI practitioner, and roboticist with 20+ years in the field. He earned his MS from NUST as a gold medalist (2006) and a PhD in Robotics and Artificial Intelligence from the Australian National University (2014), focused on autonomous aerial vehicles.

His postdoctoral work on self-driving cars at A*STAR earned a USD 15,000 prize; in July 2015 he demonstrated Singapore’s first self-driving car to Prime Minister Lee Hsien Loong. He has contributed 35+ papers, authored a computer-vision book, supervised BE/MS/PhD research, and served on the evaluation board for research funding at the National Center of Artificial Intelligence (NCAI).

He led Pakistan’s first PEC- and DRAP-approved electro-medical AI device and a team that won the PASHA ICT award in AI healthcare — with deep experience across AI, deep learning, robotics, computer vision, predictive analytics, and healthcare.

Core product

ComplyTrade

AI-based LC compliance

Automates international trade documentation for SWIFT Category 7 compliance. Ingests LC SWIFT messages (MT700, MT701, MT707, MT799), then consolidates, classifies, and verifies documents against credit terms — with discrepancy detection, audit trails, and compliance reporting.

Validated through procurement with various Pakistan's Banks.

Platform

Nexus

Autonomous recruitment

Agent-driven, voice-based interview platform that designs, conducts, and evaluates candidate interviews. Multi-agent architecture with MCP-powered sentiment analysis, real-time voice interaction, and an instant Hire/Reject scorecard with full transcript.

Deployed at Corporate Level.

Deployment

DigiArchive

Intelligent search & workflow

Intelligence layer over scanned bank and institutional archives: AI indexing, signature verification, semantic search, and automated routing — closing the digitization gap with audit trails and anomaly detection.

Pilot with the Senate of Pakistan.
